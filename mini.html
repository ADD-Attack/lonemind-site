Test bench

Ask a tiny model.

Pick a model and ask it something. Each one is small enough to run right here in your browser, and your question never leaves this page.

One short question, one short answer: facts, simple math, definitions. No chat, no memory. These are test models, so they will get things wrong.

Try: who came up with the steam engine? · what is 7 times 8? · which planet is closest to the sun?
