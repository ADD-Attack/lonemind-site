The app

An agent that lives where you do.

LoneMind is an Android app. It runs an AI model on your phone and works as your agent from there.

Alpha
Not on Google Play yet. LoneMind is in private alpha on Android. There is no public download today.
In the alpha today

What it does now.

Local models

Thinks on the device.

Download a model into the app and run it on the phone. In local mode your chats and data stay on the device.

Fit check

Knows what your phone can hold.

Each model shows whether it fits your phone's memory before you download it.

Thinking trace

Shows its work.

You see the reasoning steps under each answer as they happen.

Planned first run

One question decides the setup.

The first launch will ask how you want LoneMind to run, then set its defaults to match. You can change it later in Settings.

  1. Private. Local models only. Your data stays on the phone.
  2. More power. Adds cloud models when a task needs them.
  3. All of it. The strongest models, routed per task.
